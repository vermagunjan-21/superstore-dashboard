# Global Superstore Sales Dashboard

## Project Overview
 The Global Superstore Sales Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze sales performance across different   years, regions, categories, and customer segments. It provides valuable business insights by tracking key metrics such as Total   Sales, Profit, Orders, and Quantity sold. The dashboard helps identify trends, top-performing products, and profitable business   areas to support data-driven decision-making.
## Tech Stack
- Power BI
- Microsoft Excel (Data Cleaning)
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- Data Visualization Techniques

## Tools & Technologies
- Power BI Desktop
- DAX Functions
- Power Query Editor
- Data Modeling
- Interactive Slicers and Filters

## Data Source
- Dataset: Global Superstore Sales Dataset
- Source: Kaggle (Global Superstore Dataset)

## Dashboard Features
- KPI Cards for Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Orders, and Total Quantity.
- Interactive slicers for Year, Region, Segment, Category, and Ship Mode.
- Sales analysis by Year, Quarter, and Month.
- Sales distribution across Categories and Regions.
- Top 10 Products based on Sales.
- Customer Segment-wise Sales Analysis.
- Category-wise Profit Analysis.

  ## Business Questions
1. Which product category generates the highest sales?
2. Which region contributes the most to overall revenue?
3. What are the monthly and quarterly sales trends?
4. Which customer segment drives the highest sales?
5. What are the top-performing products?
6. Which category generates the highest profit?
7. How does sales performance vary across different years?

## Key Insights
- Technology is the leading category in terms of sales.
- Consumer segment contributes the highest sales among all customer segments.
- Quarterly sales show consistent growth, with Q4 generating the highest sales.
- Certain regions significantly outperform others in revenue generation.
- Top-selling products contribute substantially to overall business performance.
- Profitability varies across categories, highlighting opportunities for strategic planning.

## Business Impact
- Supports data-driven business decisions.
- Helps identify profitable products and markets.
- Improves sales performance monitoring.
- Enables better inventory and marketing strategies.
- Provides actionable insights for revenue growth and customer targeting.
